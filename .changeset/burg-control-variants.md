Use neutral, negative, and positive variants for buttons, form controls, Avatar, Badge, ProgressLinear, ProgressSpinner, Skeleton, Stepper, and Tags in Burg. Alert retains all five variants, and Toasts retain their message types. Remove other Burg variant overrides. When upgrading, omit unsupported variants for Burg's default appearance, or style the component in your own stylesheet to retain a custom color.
